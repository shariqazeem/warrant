## The fallback, if the venue network fails

1. **Say so, once, and move on:** "The network here has gone, so I'll show you the grant I
   issued last night."
2. **Beat 2 uses the grant issued beforehand:** its certificate is already open in a tab and
   its page is server-rendered, so it shows even from cache; the phone shows the same
   certificate over mobile data.
3. **If nothing loads at all, play the recorded video** from the laptop, from beat 3 of
   `docs/video-script.md`, and speak over it.
4. **Keep beats 5 and 6 live from memory.** They need no network.
