# Recording the demo

Two short screen recordings, no voice and no face: the only two moments that need the
company's wallet. Everything else in the films (the certificate engraving in, the counter
ticking, the public record, a certificate on a phone) is recorded from the live site with no
wallet, and the edit puts captions in the product's own words around both. Never speed up or
cut the moment a certificate engraves in or a seal presses: the continuous take is the proof.

---

## Before you start (10 minutes)

1. **The company's wallet.** Wallet B (`0xD5FE…125B`) in the OKX Wallet browser extension, on
   X Layer. It needs **$6 of USD₮0 and 0.001 OKB** for both recordings. Hold on to that much
   before moving anything else out of it. Import it privately, never with the recovery phrase
   on screen.
2. **The people.** Nothing to install: their addresses are all the recordings need.
   - A `0x223ED5D8c837dBDF38391147bB054cE7ce599d11`: chose 100% SPYx.
   - C `0x4766eb1b9ff2274bd3f819b647d69e35b05cbda9`: chose half NVDAx, half dollars.
   - D `0x2914d5fa26a1cc8faff7158070ec292c2d329df9`: chose all dollars.

   These are Warrant's own wallets, and the site says so beside every grant and payslip they
   appear on.
3. **The browser.** The one with OKX Wallet: one window, zoom 125%, bookmarks bar hidden, every
   other tab closed, **Do Not Disturb on**, Mail, Telegram and chats closed.
4. **Connect first.** Open `warrant.world/grants` and connect wallet B before you record, so the
   wallet box already shows it. (Its balance shows there; the edit blurs it. Don't worry
   about it on camera.)
5. **Recording.** ⌘⇧5 → **Record Selected Portion**, drawn around the browser window only.
   Microphone off. The OKX Wallet popups open inside that area; keep them in the frame.

## Recording 1: a grant, issued and sealed (about 60 seconds)

1. Start recording. You're on `warrant.world/grants`.
2. Paste A's address. Wait until the form says **"They chose SPYx"**.
3. Type **2** as the value. Leave SPYx.
4. Schedule: **Custom**, type **10**, choose **minutes**, no cliff.
5. Choose **Seal it** and tick the review line. The specimen certificate beside the form fills
   in as you go; give it a second.
6. Click **Issue certificate for $2.00**. In OKX Wallet: approve the signature, then confirm the
   transaction.
7. Touch nothing while the certificate engraves in. Then wait three seconds.
8. Click **Seal it now** and confirm in OKX Wallet. Wait until the seal has pressed, then five
   seconds more. Stop.

## Recording 2: three people paid in one signature (about 60 seconds)

1. Start recording on `warrant.world/run`.
2. Paste these three lines into the file box:
   ```
   0x223ED5D8c837dBDF38391147bB054cE7ce599d11, 1, Design review week 40
   0x4766eb1b9ff2274bd3f819b647d69e35b05cbda9, 1, Translated the docs
   0x2914d5fa26a1cc8faff7158070ec292c2d329df9, 1, Support rota
   ```
3. Click **Get prices for 3 people** and wait until every line has its live price.
4. Click **Pay $3 to 3 people**, approve the signature and confirm the transaction.
5. Let the payslips print, then scroll slowly through all three. Stop.

## What it costs, and where the money goes

About **$5 of USD₮0** and under a cent of OKB for both. None of it is lost: the $2 grant is in
wallet A as SPYx ten minutes later (less a 0.5% release fee to whoever releases it), and the
payroll lands in A, C and D in the same transaction, each in the split they chose. Swap the
stock back to USD₮0 in OKX Wallet whenever you like.

If a transaction fails, stop the recording, don't try again straight away, and send the error.
The product checks balances before the wallet opens and says what is missing.

## Then

Put both files in `~/Movies/warrant-demo/` and say so. From them and the live site the edit
makes:

- **the launch film's companions**: a clip of the seal pressing, and one of three payslips from
  one signature;
- **a pitch film** of about 90 seconds, with captions and no voice, for LinkedIn and as the
  finale's backup if the venue Wi-Fi fails;
- **one clip per post** on the calendar.

What the films never claim: returns, projections, user counts the public record doesn't
show, or anything about a token. The people in them are Warrant's own wallets, and the
captions say so.
