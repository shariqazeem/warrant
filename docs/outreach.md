# Outreach: inviting a team to issue a real grant

> For the founder to send, as himself, to founders and operators who pay people in USDT. Plain,
> short, no claims that aren't live. Send it once a grant can be issued from the new flow.

## The message

> Hi {name}. I'm building Warrant on X Layer: stock grants that vest, for teams that pay people in
> USDT. You pick a person, a stock (the S&P 500, NVIDIA, Apple and twelve more) and an amount; it's
> bought on day one through OKX DEX and held in an escrow nobody can spend, and it vests every
> second to them. What has vested is theirs for good.
>
> Would you issue one small real grant to one of your people this week, even $5? It takes about
> 90 seconds: warrant.world/grants. You need USDT (the USD₮0 token) and a little OKB on X Layer;
> if you don't have them I'll walk you through the withdrawal from OKX.
>
> Your person gets a certificate link that works with no wallet and ticks as it vests, and they
> can claim into OKX Wallet whenever they like. Everything is public on chain, the contracts have
> no admin, and I'll show you the transaction.
>
> I'm submitting it to OKX Dev Day on Friday, and I'd like at least one grant on it that isn't
> mine. If you're up for it, send me the wallet address of the person you'd grant to.

## What to say when they ask

- **What is the stock?** A tokenized stock issued by xStocks, a third party. It tracks the price
  and gives economic exposure; it is not a share and carries no voting rights. The issuer can
  create units, destroy units held by any address, and upgrade its contract. Not available to US
  persons, or in Canada, the UK or Australia.
- **Can I take it back?** Until you seal the grant, you can cancel the part that hasn't vested;
  what has vested stays theirs. Sealed grants can't be cancelled by anyone, including you.
- **What does it cost?** The USDT you grant, and network fees of a fraction of a cent in OKB.
  Warrant charges nothing. The release fee (0.5% by default, at most 2%) goes to whoever releases
  what's due, from the grant itself, and the person pays none when they claim it themselves.
- **Where do I see it?** Every grant has a public certificate page and a transaction on OKLink.

## Consent

- Ask before putting anyone's name in the video or in a post. Wallet addresses are public on
  chain; names are not.
- Never pay someone to say something. Real grants, real words, or nothing.
